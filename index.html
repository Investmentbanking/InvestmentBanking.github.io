About me

First-Class Computer Science Graduate (81%) from King's College London

I am a First-Class Computer Science graduate from King's College London, graduating with an overall mark of 81%. Throughout my degree, I developed a strong interest in software engineering, full-stack web development and creating user-focused applications.

My dissertation, awarded 85%, explored improving pre-send and post-send consent revocation in one-to-one messaging applications. The project involved user interviews, requirements gathering, low and high-fidelity prototyping, and the development of a frontend messaging application to evaluate new messaging features.

I enjoy building modern web applications and continuously expanding my technical skills. I am currently developing personal projects and strengthening my knowledge of software engineering, with the goal of becoming a full-stack software engineer.

Outside of technology, I enjoy gaming, tennis, football and dancing.
